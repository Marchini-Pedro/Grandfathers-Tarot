## 2.0.0 - Gunner fire rate and burst fixed: the stat recompute hook never reached minions (branch `feature/attack-timing`, third round)
The user ran `/rw_tune` on a gunner and a rifleman and the console log answered the open question. Offline only.
- **What the log showed** (`console-2026-10-02-00.03.19-*.log`): at spawn the stats were written and read back right ("renegade_rifleman: minion_num_shots_modifier x5.00 (stat now 5), ranged_attack_speed x0.25 (stat now 0.25)"), but at every burst the game read speed 1.3 and 2.25 times the base shots (gunner: 168.75 = 75 x 2.25 shots), and `/rw_tune` said "now 1.3" / "now 2.25". Those two numbers are exactly the game's `havoc_ranged_attack_speed_05` buff (`ranged_attack_speed +0.3`, `minion_num_shots_modifier x2.25`, `havoc_buff_templates.lua:2497-2504`), which the user's Havoc mods put on minions. (That it is this buff is inferred from the numbers; the buffs on the unit were not read.)
- **Cause, in our code**: the hook that puts our factor back after each recompute was installed on `BuffExtensionBase`, but the game's `class()` copies the methods of the parent into a subclass when the subclass is created (`foundation/utilities/class.lua:100-106`), so `MinionBuffExtension` keeps calling its own copy and the hook never saw a minion. Only the 0.25 s fallback timer ran, which puts the factor back for one frame in about fifteen; the burst reads at an arbitrary frame. Melee seemed to work, probably because a unit with no buff is not recomputed, so the written value stayed (inferred, not read).
- **Fix 1: hook `MinionBuffExtension._update_stat_buffs_and_keywords` too** (the same function on both classes; putting the factor back is idempotent, so a unit that reaches both is not touched twice).
- **Fix 2: a written stat is listed in `stat_buffs._modified_stats`.** The game resets only the stats listed there before a recompute (`buff_extension_base.lua:318-328`). Ours was not listed, so a buff that arrived after our write was added on top of our value and the factor ended up applied twice or lost. Now the recompute always starts from the base value and our factor is multiplied exactly once.
- Tests: a model of the game's recompute (reset the listed stats, then add additive and multiply multiplicative buffs) with the Havoc ranged buff: the unit reads 1.3 and 2.25 without the hook (the user's log), 1.3 x 0.25 and 2.25 x 5 with it, stable over repeated frames, a buff leaving or arriving later, both hooks in a row, a stat table without `_modified_stats`; a mutation check (removing the `_modified_stats` entry makes two of them fail); the entry test lists the new hook. **Not verified in game.**
- The user's idea (a shooter stimming itself raises its volley) is the same mechanism: a buff on the minion changes these two stats. We do not use a buff for it (the rule: no new buffs); the factor is applied on top of whatever buffs the unit has, so a stimmed or Havoc unit keeps its own bonus and gets ours on top (Havoc 1.3 x ours).

## 2.0.0 - Burster explosion follows its size, the animation answer, /rw_tune (branch `feature/attack-timing`, second round)
The user ran `/rw_anim` on several enemies, and reported: a burster with a custom size gets a bigger danger zone but the same blast; the gunner fire rate and shots per burst still do nothing on riflemen and scab gunners. Offline only.
- **Animation attack speed: answered, not possible cleanly.** The console log of `/rw_anim` (cultist berzerker, plague ogryn, chaos spawn): the engine has 50 `Unit` functions about animation, speed, time, scale or rate, but the animation variables found on these enemies are only the two controls (`anim_move_speed`, `moving_attack_fwd_speed`); no variable named like an attack or animation speed exists. `Unit.set_simple_animation_speed` and `crossfade_animation_set_speed` act on simple animations and crossfades, not on the state machine the enemies use. What remains is stepping the animation time by hand (`Unit.animation_set_time`) on the host only, which would show the other players a normal-speed animation under a faster hit: not built. The setting stays out.
- **Burster explosion scales with the custom size.** The danger zone is the model's own effect, so it grew with the model; the blast is built from fixed templates (`poxwalker_bomber`: radius 6 m, close radius 3 m, and the `_mild` one for a burster that dies on its own), and `BtChaosPoxwalkerExplodeAction.enter` passes no charge level or attack type that would change them (`bt_chaos_poxwalker_explode_action.lua:14,44`; the stat `explosion_radius_modifier` only counts for attack type "explosion", `explosion.lua:484-500`). A hook around `enter` swaps the action's two templates for copies with every radius multiplied by the unit's size, for the one call that makes the explosion, and restores them at once (also when the game's function raises). The blast radius is sent to the other players by the game itself (the radius travels in the explosion message), so they see it too. Damage is not scaled, only the area.
- **Gunner fire rate and shots per burst: not fixed, instrumented.** From the source both are read when a shooting burst starts (`MinionAttack.start_shooting`, `minion_attack.lua:426-453`, time between shots divided at `:589`), by every shooting action including the rifleman's and the gunner's (`bt_shoot_action.lua:388`), from the same `stat_buffs` table that the melee stat uses, which now works. Nothing in the code explains why they do nothing, so: (a) the first three units of each breed that get custom stats are written to the console log (what was written and what the stat says right after); (b) a hook after `start_shooting` writes what a tuned unit actually read (speed, number of shots, delay of the first shot) for its first three bursts per breed; (c) new command **`/rw_tune`** prints, per breed of tuned units alive, what was written, what the stats say now and what the last burst read, to chat and console log. Run it on riflemen with fire rate 25 and burst 500 after they have shot, and send the log: it tells whether the stats are written, whether they survive, and whether the game reads them.

## 2.0.0 - Time between attacks, chains kept whole, animation probe (branch `feature/attack-timing`, not merged into `main` until the user confirms it works in game)
The user's second test of the custom mods: a Plague Ogryn at 250 percent stopped its chained attacks after the first hit, the Chaos Spawn's multi-attack too; and "Melee attack speed" said "how soon its next swing comes", which is a time between attacks, so it should be called that, with a separate attack speed that makes the animation itself faster if that can be done per unit. Offline only.
- **Cause of the cut chains (game source).** `BtMeleeAttackAction._start_attack_anim` ends a melee attack at `t + max(duration / melee_attack_speed, T + 0.27 s)`; for a chained sweep `T` is the end of the FIRST hit, not the last (`bt_melee_attack_action.lua:259-271`; the non-sweep branch correctly uses the last, `:336-348`). Plague Ogryn combo: 3.56 s long, first hit ends at 1.21 s, last at 2.84 s; at 250 percent the attack ended at 1.48 s, before its second hit (1.81 s). The Chaos Spawn combo does the same. The game never met it: its own values are 1.3-1.4. A hook after `_start_attack_anim` (`Tuning.fix_attack_end`) now uses the end of the LAST hit for units with a custom time between attacks, and only lengthens an attack. The speed does not touch the swings themselves, only the wait that follows them, so a chain can never get shorter than its last hit plus 0.27 s.
- **"Melee attack speed" is now "Time between attacks"** (id `gap`): a time in percent (50 = half the wait, 200 = twice as long), 25 to 400, written to the game's stat as `100 / value`. The number now goes the same way as the name. Cards, shared cards, presets and recipes written with the old names (`melee`, `melee speed`, `melee attack speed`, `melee attack`, `attack speed`) are read as the new setting with the number converted (`melee=200` becomes `gap=50`) so they behave as before, and are written back with the new name. New aliases: `gap`, `time between attacks`, `attack gap`, `attack time`, `attack delay`, `attack interval`.
- **Animation attack speed: not built, a probe instead.** No script of the game changes the speed of a minion's animation (docs/07). New console command **`/rw_anim`** (host, with a wave unit alive): lists the engine's `Unit` functions that mention animation, speed, time, scale or rate and, per breed, which candidate animation variables the unit's state machine has (with their range; `anim_move_speed` and `moving_attack_fwd_speed` are included as controls). ## 2.0.0 - step 2: the tarot draw (in progress, see docs/05)
- **New default draw mode "Tarot draw"** (`mode = tarot`; the old modes stay as "Random countdown (host rolls)" and "Votes (players choose, legacy)", an unknown value means tarot). Every cycle the host waits for the interval, then **deals a hand** of 1-5 cards (option `tarot_cards`, default 4) **`tarot_seconds` (5-30, default 10) before the pick**, weighted by the chance weights, **no card twice in one hand**, only cards that are enabled, have enemies and are **not cooling down**. The **winner is chosen when the hand is dealt**, uniformly among the dealt cards (the weights decide who is dealt, not who wins), and synced with the hand, so clients can animate a roulette that lands on the host's card. A hand of one card has no roulette. At the pick the winner's wave spawns, the next interval starts at once, and the resolved hand stays in the state for 16 s (`drawn`) so every screen can play the reveal and the rot. An interval shorter than the hand time deals at once and the hand is as long as the interval.
- **Cooldowns are strict now**: a drawn card is out of EVERY later hand for its full cooldown (the cooldown clock `cd_clock` counts only played time, so `/rw_pause`, an empty server and the loading screens do not run it). With nothing eligible the state says why (`e` = 1 nothing in the draw, 2 every card is cooling down) and the director tries again every 10 s. `/rw_skip` while waiting deals and picks at once, `/rw_next` throws a hand away without spawning, anti-snowballing delays the pick and lengthens the fuse of a hand that is showing. The legacy modes use the same played-time clock.
- **Protocol 2 / version 2.0.0** (a 1.x peer is refused by the handshake, as always). The state JSON gained, for tarot: `h` (the hand: `{k key, n name, s suit, t threat, b enemies (max 8), q whisper, m modifiers, r rare}` per card, no percentage), `w` (winner index), `sq` (hand sequence number: a new number means a new hand to animate), `dn` (resolved), `y` (seconds the hand lasts), `cd` (seconds left per cooling card, so the Deck screen of a client can show the cooldown looks) and `e` 0/1/2. Clients validate everything (at most 5 cards, unknown suit becomes plague, threat 1-5, strings cut, the winner is pulled into the hand) and count the cooldowns down locally (frozen while the host is paused).
- **Options**: the mode dropdown (default Tarot draw), `Cards drawn` and `Seconds before the pick` in the timing group.
